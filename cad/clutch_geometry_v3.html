Motcore v3 — O-ring Clutch Geometry (Y-Z cross-section)

A 8°
B 35 mm
R 20 mm
d 2.5 mm
θ 8°
v3: output shaft carries a disc (radius R) with an O-ring in a groove on its rim.
At engagement (θ = A) the O-ring presses against the flat face of the motor disc (also radius R).
Both discs share the same radius R. Motor shaft is vertical; output shaft tilts from UJ.
