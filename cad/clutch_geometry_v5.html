Cross-section in the vertical plane containing the motor axis (Z) and the output shaft axis (Y). The output cone rides a carriage that translates vertically; the pinion is rigid to the cone and lives permanently inside the corona, so it is captive and cannot fall out. Free = pinion concentric with the corona (centre distance 0, output shaft carries nothing). Descending the carriage drives the pinion toward the corona teeth, then brings the interleaved rubber rings into flank-to-flank contact, then preloads them. Preload travel is absorbed by the gear's own root clearance (0.25·m) — no slot, no floating ring.
